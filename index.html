A summer in the Tien Shan

Bugu

You are a boy spending the summer at your grandmother’s yurt, high in a valley of the Tien Shan, the Celestial Mountains. Spruce forests climb the slopes, marmots whistle on the pastures and, somewhere among the rocks, a snow leopard keeps watch.

Wander where you like. Gather wild apples and edelweiss, and find the valley’s ten secret places.
